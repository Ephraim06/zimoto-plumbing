Common questions

Quick answers about emergencies, service areas and getting a quote.

Do you offer after-hours emergency plumbing?

Yes. We provide after-hours assistance for urgent problems such as burst pipes and burst geysers. Call or WhatsApp +27 81 761 8312.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Cape Town and surrounding areas, including Durbanville, Brackenfell, Stellenbosch, Table View, Parklands, Cape Town CBD, Sea Point, Kuilsriver, Bellville, Goodwood, Plattekloof, Milnerton, Woodstock, Claremont and Constantia.

What plumbing services do you provide?

Our services include burst pipe repairs, burst and new geyser installations, blocked drains, bathroom renovations, new plumbing projects, maintenance and general repairs.
